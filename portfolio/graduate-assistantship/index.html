Research Support for an Unfinished UX Textbook

TL;DR

I helped Cathy Smith with research for a UX textbook that remains incomplete. The public evidence here is a set of research-support and document artifacts: an inventory of sources and course materials, a review of MS UX program web copy, and an instructional draft about learning Figma with AI.

The actual role

This was a graduate assistantship in Kent State's School of Information from August 2024 through May 2025. My confirmed contribution was helping Cathy Smith research her textbook. The manuscript is unfinished. The page stays with document-level evidence and does not infer adoption, measured outcomes, or a completed study.

The work was document work: gathering and organizing source material, inspecting how UX-program information was structured and written, and shaping an instructional explanation of a practical learning approach. That is useful evidence of synthesis and communication without turning support into authorship.

Artifacts that exist

Book-analysis inventory

A working inventory organized research relevant to the unfinished textbook. It shows source gathering and organization; it is not a published chapter or a claim that the manuscript was completed.

Course-resource and lecture inventory

A structured pass over course resources and lecture material, used to make the available instructional landscape easier to inspect. Internal course material is not reproduced here.

MS UX program web-copy review

A site-map and copy review of the MS UX program website. The artifact records content structure and language questions, not a measured enrollment or conversion result.

Figma-with-AI instructional draft

An instructional draft explains a self-guided approach: use screenshots and an AI tutor to learn inside a real Figma task, while recognizing that results vary. It does not claim measured learning gains for a class.

What this demonstrates

I can gather source material, organize a document system, inspect content for clarity, and explain a technical learning approach. Faculty supervision and the unfinished manuscript set the boundary around that evidence.

What this page does not claim

The page makes no claim of textbook authorship, publication, coding-framework ownership, reliability duties, a completed manuscript, or an institutional outcome. The examples are concise descriptions of permission-safe work, not a wholesale release of internal course or research files.
