Summary

Interaction designer and creative technologist turning complex research and musical practices into working tools. Leads product direction, research, design, and evaluation, using AI coding tools for implementation. Completing an MS in User Experience at Kent State University.

Selected Projects

Clarence AI Collaboration and Memory System →2025–Present
  • Created a persistent AI collaboration system for ongoing research and product development, connecting searchable memory, a project wiki, and specialist agents to carry work across sessions and between Linux and Mac.
  • Designed controlled memory updates with a single writing agent and source history; connect local and hosted models through shared retrieval and Model Context Protocol (MCP) integrations.
  • In a fixed 75-question evaluation in May 2026, wiki-first routing increased queries with relevant results in the top 20 from 23 to 73. Continue investigating missed context and testing recovery when components fail.
SensorSynthFM →2024–Present
  • Conceived and lead development of a touch-native iPad FM performance instrument, exploring how multitouch interaction, device motion, and environmental sensing can shape sound and performance through the platform’s distinctive affordances.
  • Designed held-note editing so players can sustain a chord, retarget or merge notes, and remove individual pitches; verified behavior on a physical iPad prototype built with Swift/SwiftUI and AudioKit.
  • MS capstone: five-participant usability discovery study planned to guide refinement; continued development and release planned after graduation.
KB POMO →2026
  • Designed and released KB POMO on the App Store, integrating kettlebell exercise scheduling, demonstrations, and workout logging with Apple Watch and Apple Health.
  • Built with SwiftUI; an Android port using Kotlin and Jetpack Compose is in testing.
Rhythm Dice and Cymbal Phrase →2026
  • Designed two free, open source Max for Live tools that generate rhythm and cymbal patterns from musical rules; directed listening tests and verification in Ableton Live.
  • Published reproducible packages with 32 automated tests across both tools. Rhythm Dice passed Live testing; Cymbal Phrase is a public beta.
