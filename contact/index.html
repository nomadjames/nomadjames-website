Hiring and scoped collaboration

Bring a product decision

I am open to UX research, interaction design, human-AI interaction, agentic UX, and narrowly scoped creative-tool work. A useful first message gives enough context to judge fit without turning the form into a pitch deck.

Include these five things

  • The role or product problem
  • The intended users
  • The timeline
  • Relevant links or artifacts
  • The decision that must be made

I will reply with an honest fit or no-fit assessment.
