iPad music study

Participant questionnaire replica · AI-assisted research artifact

These questions help select participants for an in-person study of a prototype iPad musical instrument. The session takes up to 60 minutes, the iPad is provided, and participants receive a $25 gift card for their time. Completing this questionnaire does not book a session or earn a gift card. Participation is voluntary. No previous synthesizer or music-making experience is needed. Audio and video recording is required to take part.
