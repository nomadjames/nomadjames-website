A tree is a diagram of attention.

In this study, a branch is not decoration. It is a decision repeated at a smaller scale: continue, split, or stop. The orchard begins from the same seed every time, but your movement makes each visit a different reading of that seed.

At the base, chlorophyll marks the living path. At the crown, tiny ochre fruits appear only where a line has survived three generations of listening. The dark pinpricks are not stars; they are held breaths.
