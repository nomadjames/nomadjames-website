KB POMO Support

Questions, bugs or ideas: reach me through nomadjames.com/contact.

Common questions

The alarm didn't ring. Open the app once. It resets the alarms for the rest of your workout. Then check that alarms are on in Settings, under KB POMO.

My sets aren't in Apple Health. Only timed sets go to Health. Tap Start before a set and Stop when you finish. Then check that KB POMO is allowed in the Health app, under Sharing, then Apps.

A demo video is gone. Tap thumbs down to see another pick, or tap Search YouTube.

The watch app is empty. Open KB POMO on your phone while the watch app is open.

How do I save my history? In History, tap Export. It makes a spreadsheet you can keep or share.

An exercise doesn't suit my space or my body.On the set's card, tap Swap exercise. The app suggests another that works the same way, or you can pick any exercise.

Safety

Read the terms of use before you train.

Privacy

KB POMO keeps your data on your devices. Read the privacy policy.
