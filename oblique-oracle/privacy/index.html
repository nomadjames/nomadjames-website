Oblique Oracle Privacy Policy

Last updated October 5, 2026.

Oblique Oracle has no account, no analytics and no ads. It doesn't save your questions or readings.

What leaves your phone

To write a reading, the app sends your question, the hexagrams you cast and the card you drew to a small server I run on Cloudflare. That server passes them to Groq, which runs the AI model, and streams the reading back. My server doesn't store them. Cloudflare's and Groq's privacy policies apply to how they handle the request.

What stays on your phone

Nothing is saved. Casting the coins and drawing the card happen on the phone, and each reading is gone when you ask again or close the app.

Children

Oblique Oracle is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date.
